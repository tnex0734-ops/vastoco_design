# 2–3 minute walkthrough outline

## 0:00–0:20 — Context

“Vastoco serves two audiences with different needs: clients looking for verified design professionals, and professionals looking to showcase their work and offer consultations. We designed two separate homepage doors under one consistent brand.”

## 0:20–0:55 — Client door

Show `/clients` at desktop, then mobile.

- Point out the clear client-focused headline and primary search action.
- Explain discovery by architect, interior designer and Vastu consultant.
- Show the comparison information hierarchy: portfolio, experience, fee and reviews.
- Explain the verification, secure-payment and genuine-review trust language.
- Show how the layout is recomposed for 390px.

## 0:55–1:30 — Professional door

Show `/professionals` at desktop, then mobile.

- Point out the distinct professional value proposition.
- Highlight free joining and the 85% consultation fee share.
- Explain client briefs and verified public profiles.
- Explain that architect verification includes a COA check.
- Show the visible link back to the client door.

## 1:30–2:10 — Supporting interactions

- Show role selection only when signup intent is unknown.
- Show the client consultation details flow.
- Show the professional onboarding stepper, conditional COA field, portfolio upload preview and pending-verification state.
- State clearly that this is a frontend prototype: no accounts, payments or credential checks are live.

## 2:10–2:40 — Visual and engineering choices

- Explain the strict Espresso/Pale Oak two-colour system, the monochrome Espresso Vastoco wordmark, Playfair Display + Inter pairing and duotone architectural illustration style.
- Show the gateway monitor-stage hero and mention that reduced-motion preference is respected.
- Explain the lightweight dependency-free frontend, responsive layout and accessibility decisions.

## 2:40–3:00 — Close

“Both doors have one shared identity, but each speaks to a distinct audience and makes the next action clear. The implementation is responsive and frontend-only, with supporting flows to demonstrate how the experience could continue beyond the homepages.”

Do not claim that features are live, that professionals are already verified, or that bookings/payments are functional.
