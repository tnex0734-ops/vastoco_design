# Taste Engine Reference Scan

## Reference 1: Lars Müller Publishers (Archive & Monograph Platform)
- **Concept**: Rigorous Swiss modernist monograph catalog where layout geometry and severe margins showcase physical architectural books.
- **Fonts**: Helvetica Neue / Akzidenz Grotesk with generous line-height and tight tabular indices.
- **Palette**: Warm cream paper base with dense charcoal/black ink and minimal structural lines.
- **Motion**: Subtle page transitions, zero scroll bounce, instant filter switches.
- **Craft Detail**: Technical book dimensions and ISBN metadata displayed in crisp, tabular micro-type alongside full-bleed project plates.

## Reference 2: El Croquis (Architectural Monograph Editions)
- **Concept**: Deep-dive technical monograph archive for master architects (Moneo, Koolhaas, Siza, SANAA).
- **Fonts**: Classic serif display with strict, neutral modern sans body copy.
- **Palette**: Architectural linen, parchment cream, deep warm brown/black ink.
- **Motion**: Stately section reveals that mimic turning heavy architectural paper pages.
- **Craft Detail**: Technical line drawings (plans, elevations, sections) juxtaposed against high-contrast black-and-white site photography.

## Reference 3: Studio Mumbai / Bijoy Jain (Architectural Archive)
- **Concept**: Tactile, material-first documentation of Indian architecture, craft traditions, and environmental context.
- **Fonts**: Understated humanist sans with wide letter-spacing on subheads.
- **Palette**: Earth pigments, natural limestone, deep raw umber, warm muted sand.
- **Motion**: Unhurried fade transitions; imagery commands complete contemplative focus.
- **Craft Detail**: Emphasis on unadorned materials, joinery details, and natural light casting authentic shadows.

## Reference 4: Are.na (Index & Channel Architecture)
- **Concept**: Brutalist, no-bullshit index system where information density and direct manipulation replace marketing clutter.
- **Fonts**: Strict monospace and utilitarian system grotesque.
- **Palette**: High-contrast black on white with muted gray dividing rules.
- **Motion**: Instantaneous state responses; zero decorative animation.
- **Craft Detail**: Dense, border-aligned metadata grids that allow rapid comparison of diverse media.
