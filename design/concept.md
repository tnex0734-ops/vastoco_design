# Taste Engine Concept Document & Idea Burn

## 1. Five Big Ideas
- **Idea 1 (Eliminated / Slop Burn)**: A luxury curated directory with full-bleed hero video and floating architect cards with golden stars. (Reason for deletion: Generic template pattern, passive browsing without bilateral marketplace action).
- **Idea 2 (Eliminated / Slop Burn)**: An on-demand service app where homeowners request "instant home quotes" from nearby contractors. (Reason for deletion: Commoditizes architecture, degrades professional dignity into gig work).
- **Idea 3 (Candidate)**: **The Architectural Monograph Ledger**. The platform acts as a dual-sided bound ledger where projects are treated as peer-reviewed monographs and client briefs are registered as formal commissions.
- **Idea 4 (Candidate)**: **The Delhi NCR Design Dispatch**. A high-speed drafting table where homeowners post architectural briefs and licensed practitioners reply with structured consultation proposals.
- **Idea 5 (Candidate)**: **The Master Studio Consultation Salon**. A private, appointment-only salon interface focused strictly on verified video dialogues and transparent 85/15 fee payouts.

## 2. Selection & Tiebreaker
Applying our rolled extra constraint (**crisp architectural box geometry & no rounded pill-shaped generic buttons**), **Idea 3 (The Architectural Monograph Ledger)** is selected. It enforces structural rectilinear discipline, tabular drafting borders, and authentic architectural documentation across both client discovery and professional workspace workflows.

## 3. The Core Concept
> **This site is an architectural monograph and bilateral project ledger that transforms chaotic Indian design hiring into a verified, dignified exchange between cultured homeowners and master practitioners.**

## 4. Three Memorable Moments
1. **The Authentic Architect-at-Work Hero & Split Portal**: Instead of generic seated clipart, visitors are greeted by authentic photography of an architect at a drafting table with balsa wood models, flanked by transparent two-sided gateway actions ("Explore Verified Professionals" vs "Join as a Practicing Professional").
2. **The 85/15 Fee Transparency Calculator in the Offer Composer**: When a professional composes a consultation proposal for a client brief, an interactive breakdown visibly displays the client fee, the exact 85% practitioner share, and the 15% platform commission deduction — establishing total bilateral trust.
3. **The Interactive Calendar & Conflict Detection Matrix**: In the authenticated professional workspace, consultations are rendered in a clean, drafting-inspired agenda and time-grid that immediately flags scheduling overlaps and provides one-click launch for verified video sessions.
