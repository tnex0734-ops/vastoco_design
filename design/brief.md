# Vastoco Product Brief — Two-Sided Architecture & Design Marketplace

## 1. What It Is
Vastoco is an authoritative, high-trust two-sided architectural, interior design, and Vastu consultancy marketplace operating across Delhi NCR (Noida, Gurugram, South Delhi, and Faridabad). 

Unlike unstructured lead-generation directories or commoditized gig portals, Vastoco functions as a curated digital monograph and transaction hub that balances the needs of both sides:
1. **Homeowners & Clients**: Discover vetted architects, interior designers, and Vastu consultants; compare verified credentials (such as Council of Architecture / COA registration); review authentic project monographs; post structured project briefs with granular privacy controls (Private, Selected Pros, or Open); and book paid 15, 30, or 45-minute video consultations.
2. **Practicing Professionals**: Showcase peer-reviewed portfolios; receive qualified client briefs matching their discipline, locality, and budget parameters; dispatch tailored consultation proposals/pitches; manage their consultation calendar; and retain 85% of consultation fees (with a transparent 15% platform commission clearly stated).

## 2. Audience Profiles
### Side A: The Client / Homeowner
- **Demographics**: Age 28–58; modern homeowners, villa builders, penthouse owners, and luxury apartment renovators across Delhi NCR.
- **Mindset**: Cultured, discerning, time-conscious; distrustful of contractor commissions, generic contractor directories, and unverified claims.
- **Core Need**: Verified credentials, authentic photographic portfolios (not stock renders), clear scope pricing, and an easy, pressure-free way to speak directly with the principal architect before signing multi-lakh execution contracts.

### Side B: The Architecture & Design Professional
- **Demographics**: Independent architectural practices, boutique interior design studios, and senior Vastu practitioners.
- **Mindset**: Value professional dignity, despise cold pitching, hate chasing low-intent tire-kickers.
- **Core Need**: High-intent client briefs with confirmed budgets and timelines; paid consultation bookings that respect billable hours; an editorial portfolio presentation worthy of an architectural monograph; and clean administrative tools to send proposals and manage schedules.

## 3. Feeling in the First 3 Seconds
Quiet authority, material permanence, and architectural poise. The interface feels like opening an exquisite cloth-bound monograph from an independent architectural press — tactile Pale Oak `#DDD9CE` linen, deep Espresso `#3C2415` typography, sharp rhythmic alignment, and zero synthetic ornamentation.

## 4. Single Product Goal
Create a balanced, frictionless exchange where clients post structured briefs and book verified consultations, and professionals browse opportunities and dispatch high-trust proposals — all within a unified, strictly brand-compliant two-colour design system.

## 5. What It Must Never Look Like
- **No Aggregator Directory Slop**: No badge-cluttered search grids, no star-spamming popups, no "Get 5 Free Quotes Now" lead-capture forms.
- **No SaaS Dashboard Generic Clichés**: No purple/blue neon gradients, no floating glowing orbs, no dark-mode gold champagne luxury tropes, no vanity metrics counters.
- **No Asymmetric Exploitation**: No hidden commissions, no free spec-work expectations, no exposing client private contact information to unsolicited callers.

## 6. Technical Stack & Brand Constraints
- **Core Stack**: Vanilla ES Modules, native modern DOM APIs, CSS Custom Properties, semantic HTML5, zero external build overhead.
- **Motion & Scroll**: GSAP Core + ScrollTrigger, Lenis smooth scrolling, CSS hardware-accelerated transforms and opacity.
- **Typography**: Playfair Display (editorial authority & serif poise) paired with Inter (utilitarian precision & tabular numbers).
- **Strict Brand Palette**: Exclusively Espresso `#3C2415` and Pale Oak `#DDD9CE`. No pure black `#000000`, pure white `#FFFFFF`, gold `#D4AF37`, green, or orange UI elements.
